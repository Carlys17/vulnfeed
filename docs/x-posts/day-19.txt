Day 3 of the @Telegraphprotoc hackathon: Chainguard is live.

A watchlist app that monitors Base contracts in real time, powered by VulnFeed (ONCHAIN_TX_LOOKUP). Add any contract address → rating, risk score and findings, re-checked every 60s.
